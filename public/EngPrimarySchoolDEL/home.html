English Higher Primary School

OverviewStaffFacilitiesContactAll Institutions

Primary school runs in the main block of the school from 1st standard to VII standard; it has three sections in each class and total strength is 1000. Every year we get 100% result at VII standard public examination with a number of 1st classes. The good performance at the examination is a result of the able guidance of the management and hard work of the members of the staff of the Institution. This has attracted many pupils from far off places of the city. Thirty qualified, experienced and dedicated teachers are engaged for the primary section. A rolling cup instituted in the name of Mr C.M. Aleemulla Khan is awarded to the highest marks scoring girl at the VII standard Public Examination. Library and laboratory facilities are provided to the students. Girls participate in the indoor games within campus.

Strength (1000), number of teachers (thirty) and results are of the time of writing and are not current figures.

Naveeda Naaz (M.A, B.Sc, B.Ed)Head Mistress

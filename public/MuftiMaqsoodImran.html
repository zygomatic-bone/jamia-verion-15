About Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi

Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi is a prominent Islamic scholar, educator and community leader who serves as the Chief Imam and Khateeb of Jamia Masjid at City Market, Bengaluru, one of the largest mosques in Karnataka. He leads the congregation, delivers the Friday Khutbah and provides religious guidance to the community. His sermons, lectures and religious talks are published on his official YouTube channel, @MuftiMaqsoodImran.

Education and scholarly journey

After completing his tenth-grade education, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi memorised the Holy Quran in approximately 18 months. He subsequently completed a seven-year Aalim course alongside studies in Persian (Farsi). In 2001, he earned an M.A. in Urdu, followed by a Ph.D. researching the life and poetic works of Moulana Mufti Ashraf Ali.

His scholarly work brings together traditional Islamic learning, religious guidance, education and community service. His lectures address Quranic studies, Islamic jurisprudence, Tafseer, Hadith, Seerat-un-Nabi and contemporary social issues.

In 2000, he became Naib Imam at Jamia Masjid, and in 2011 he was appointed Permanent Imam and Khateeb. Jamia Masjid stands on S.J.P. Road, City Market, Bangalore – 560002, on the campus of Jame-ul-Uloom, the group of institutions managed by the Jamia Masjid & Muslim Charitable Fund Trust.

Religious leadership and state-level responsibilities

Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi holds executive responsibilities within Karnataka's Islamic religious and community leadership. Following the passing of Ameer-e-Shariat Maulana Sagheer Ahmed Khan Rashadi in January 2026, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi became the principal authority and a key driving member of the three-member caretaker council, known as Majlis-e-Imarat Shariya, constituted to administer the affairs of Imarat-e-Shariah, Karnataka. Exercising significant administrative and public influence within this supreme leadership body, he stands at the forefront of steering statewide Shariah directives, spearheading major organisational conventions (Ijlas), issuing official religious edicts, and serving as a primary voice mobilising ulema, imams and community institutions across Karnataka towards unity, constitutional engagement and disciplined governance.

He also serves as Convener of the Markazi Ruyat-e-Hilal (Karnataka Moon-Sighting) Committee. In this role, he coordinates moon-sighting activities and communicates decisions concerning the beginning of Islamic months, including Ramadan, Shawwal and Dhul-Hijjah.

Educational leadership and student development

Alongside his religious responsibilities, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi serves as Principal of Jamiul Uloom Arabic College and the Jamia-ul-Uloom Group of Institutions, as well as Rector of Jame-ul-Uloom Shaheen Academy.

His educational work combines traditional Islamic learning with opportunities for modern academic development. He established an educational department supporting hundreds of students through residential, day-scholar and NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling) pathways, with programmes spanning the 10th standard, PUC and degree studies.

The initiative integrates Hafiz-e-Quran and Aalim courses with foundational education and preparation for competitive examinations such as NEET, JEE and UPSC. The programme has recorded a 100% pass rate for four consecutive years. Through these efforts, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi works to widen educational opportunities, support students from disadvantaged backgrounds and encourage young people to pursue higher education.

International engagement and interfaith dialogue

Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi has represented the Indian Muslim scholarly community through religious and intercultural engagements in the United States, Iran, Iraq and Saudi Arabia.

For nearly 14 years, he travelled to the United States during Ramadan to lead prayers, recite the Quran and deliver sermons at the invitation of American institutions. His experiences there have informed his emphasis on peaceful coexistence, productive communities and dialogue across cultural and religious differences.

In Tehran, Iran, he represented India at the International Peace and Brotherhood Conference, promoting Islamic unity and dialogue. His scholarly and spiritual outreach has also included visits to important Islamic centres in Iraq, including Baghdad, Najaf and Karbala, as well as engagements connected with Saudi Arabia.

Through these international experiences, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi has emphasised brotherhood, moderation, mutual respect and cooperation among communities.

Community service and peacebuilding in Bengaluru

Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi is recognised for his community engagement and interfaith outreach in Bengaluru. He serves as a Patron of Markaz Tahaffuz-e-Islam Hind and has been described publicly as a “Peacebuilder of Bengaluru”.

Drawing on the principles of the Prophet's Meesaq-e-Madina, he encourages dialogue and cooperation among people of different faiths. His approach includes welcoming Hindu swamis, Christian priests and Dalit leaders to Jamia Masjid for discussions, encouraging peaceful relations between Hindu and Muslim traders in K.R. Market, and working with religious leaders on equitable loudspeaker regulations.

During local controversies involving religious banners, his message has emphasised that love and respect are demonstrated through conduct and unity rather than symbols alone. He has also supported the #TruthInMedia youth campaign against media sensationalism, organised peaceful human chains concerning Waqf matters and advocated constitutional engagement in addressing community concerns.

Following the 2025 Pahalgam terror attack, Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi condemned terrorism and stressed that it has no place in religion. His message, “Terror has no religion—we love Hindustan,” expressed his commitment to peace, justice and national unity.

English Nursery School

OverviewStaffFacilitiesContactAll Institutions

Starting of nursery school was the first step of endeavour in the field of education. It took its birth in the year 1984 with just 2 pupils in a thatched structure. It was an eventful beginning. The architects of the endeavour were the trustees of the Institution and Moulana Riyazur Rahman Rashadi Saheb, the Principal. The Institution continued to expand by leaps and bounds. Now the strength has grown to around 500; special attention is given for punctuality and cleanliness. Moral Education commences from this stage. The pupils start learning Hadiths from this stage.

Dates differ between pages. This page gives 1984 for the start of the nursery; the Founder Secretary’s essay gives 1986 and the Admissions Diary 1983-84. “Around 500” pupils is the figure of the time of writing.

Speaker

Mufti Dr. Mohamed Maqsood Imran Rashadi

  1. 1

    Chief Imam & Khateeb

    Jamia Masjid, City Market, Bengaluru.

    Leads the congregation and delivers the Friday Khutbah and religious guidance.

  2. 2

    Principal

    Jamiul Uloom Arabic College, Bengaluru.

    Provides educational leadership and oversees academic, religious and character development.

  3. 3

    Member, Imarat-e-Shariah, Karnataka

    Three-Member Caretaker Council.

    Contributes to the religious, educational and community responsibilities of Imarat-e-Shariah, Karnataka.

  4. 4

    Convener, Karnataka Moon Sighting Committee

    Coordinates moon-sighting activities and communicates decisions concerning the commencement of Islamic months, including Ramadan, Shawwal and Dhul-Hijjah.

  5. 5

    Islamic Scholar, Educator & Community Leader

    Engages in Islamic scholarship, teaching, public lectures, community guidance and youth mentorship.

  6. 6

    Ameer-e-Shariyat, Karnataka (Member)

    Serves as a member of the Ameer-e-Shariyat leadership structure, contributing to religious guidance and community responsibilities in Karnataka.

AREAS OF WORK

  • Quranic Studies
  • Islamic Jurisprudence
  • Religious Education
  • Public Sermons
  • Community Guidance
  • Interfaith Dialogue
  • Moon-Sighting Coordination
  • Student Development

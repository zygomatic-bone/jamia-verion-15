What the Trust’s pages say about the J.U Darul Quran Arabic College, Bangalore:

Now we pray with Almighty to all those who are actively involved in bringing up J.U Arabic College at Bannikuppe. This institution comes up very fast so as to serve the community in a big way.

The management could succeed in getting 61 acres of land, which is 25kms away from the City Mosque at Bannikuppa, so that, a part of it can be rescued for Arabic Studies, in which the construction has already begun … to accommodate lecture hall. It accommodates Lecture Hall, Mosque, Library, Guest house & a Kitchen, Computer center, Tailoring and calligraphy center.
